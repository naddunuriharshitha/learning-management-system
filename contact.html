Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions about Great Minds.

What is Great Minds?

Great Minds is an educational website that provides information about remarkable personalities and their contributions.

What type of leaders can I explore?

You can explore Indian leaders, scientists, social reformers, technology leaders, sports personalities, writers, artists and world leaders.

Can I suggest a leader?

Yes. You can use the contact form to send your suggestions for personalities that could be included on the website.

Is Great Minds free to use?

Yes. The website is designed as a free educational platform for exploring information about great personalities.

Can I provide corrections?

Yes. If you notice incorrect or outdated information, you can use the contact form to let us know.
