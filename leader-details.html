About Mahatma Gandhi

Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, popularly known as Mahatma Gandhi, was an Indian lawyer, politician, social activist and leader. He played a major role in India's struggle for independence from British rule.

Gandhi promoted non-violence, truth and peaceful resistance. His methods influenced India's freedom movement and inspired movements for civil rights and freedom around the world.

Education

Gandhi studied law in London and became a barrister. His education and experiences helped shape his understanding of law, society and justice.

Career & Journey

Gandhi initially worked as a lawyer. During his years in South Africa, he developed important ideas about peaceful resistance and equality.

After returning to India, he became one of the major leaders of the Indian independence movement.

Major Contributions

  • Promoted the principle of Ahimsa.
  • Developed the method of Satyagraha.
  • Played a major role in India's freedom movement.
  • Supported social equality and community development.
  • Inspired peaceful movements around the world.

Major Movements

Non-Cooperation Movement

A major movement encouraging people to peacefully resist British rule.

Salt March

Gandhi led the historic Salt March as a peaceful protest against the British salt laws.

Quit India Movement

Gandhi supported the movement calling for an end to British rule in India.
